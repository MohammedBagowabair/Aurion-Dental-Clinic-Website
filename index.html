Treatments

Everything your smile needs, under one roof

Swipe through our core treatments. Each one starts with an examination and a conversation about what is right for you.

01

Check-ups & scale and polish

A thorough examination of teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues, with digital X-rays only when clinically needed, followed by gentle scaling and polishing. You leave with a clear, plain-language summary of what we saw.

Every 6 months for most adults

02

Fillings & restorative care

Tooth-coloured fillings, inlays and onlays to repair decay, chips and worn edges. We explain each option, how long it is expected to last and how to care for it, before any work begins.

Usually one visit

03

Root canal treatment

When the nerve of a tooth is inflamed or infected, root canal treatment can often save it. We use rubber-dam isolation and magnification, and take time to keep you comfortable throughout.

1–3 visits, case dependent

04

Crowns, bridges & veneers

Restorations planned with digital scans and shade matching, aiming for a natural look that suits your face and bite. We discuss what is realistic for your teeth rather than promising a fixed result.

2–3 visits

Clear aligners & orthodontics

A consultation, scan and treatment plan for clear aligners or fixed braces, with regular reviews. Suitability depends on your teeth and gums, and we will tell you honestly if another approach fits better.

Plan reviewed every 6–8 weeks

Your first visit

What to expect, step by step

  1. 1

    Message us on WhatsApp

    Tell us what you need and your preferred times. Our front desk replies during opening hours to suggest a slot.

  2. 2

    Arrive 10 minutes early

    Bring your IC or passport, a list of any medicines and previous X-rays if you have them. We will ask a few health questions.

  3. 3

    Unhurried examination

    Your dentist checks teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues, and takes images only if they are clinically needed.

  4. 4

    A clear plan, in writing

    We explain findings on screen, outline the options, including doing nothing, and give you a written plan to take home.

  5. 5

    Treatment at your pace

    Nothing is started on the day unless you are comfortable. You are welcome to take time, ask questions or seek a second opinion.

Please bring: IC or passport, medication list, insurance or panel card, previous dental X-rays if available.

Technology & hygiene

Clean you can see. Care you can feel.

Our sterilisation room follows a one-way flow from dirty to clean, and every cycle is tested and logged. Ask us to show you around.

  • Class B autoclave sterilisation with every cycle logged
  • Single-use needles, bibs, cups and suction tips
  • Sealed instrument pouches opened in front of you
  • Low-dose digital X-rays and intraoral scanning
  • Surface disinfection and air filtration between patients
  • Magnification loupes for precise, conservative work

Patient guide

Questions we hear every week

Do I need a referral?

No. You can message us directly on WhatsApp to request an appointment. If you have been referred, please bring the letter and any images.

How do I request an appointment?

Tap any WhatsApp button. A message is pre-written for you; add your preferred days and times. We do not store any booking data on this website.

Is treatment painful?

Most treatment is done under local anaesthetic, and we check in with you throughout. Tell us if you are anxious; we can plan shorter visits and agree a stop signal.

Do you accept insurance or company panels?

Coverage depends on your provider and plan. Please send us your insurer or employer name on WhatsApp and our team will let you know what may apply before your visit.

What if I have a dental emergency?

During opening hours, WhatsApp us and we will try to see you the same day. For facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding or serious injury, call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Can I bring my child to my appointment?

Yes, and we are happy to book family visits back-to-back. For safety, children should stay with an adult in the waiting area during your treatment.

Health tips

Brushing · 3 min read

Two minutes, twice a day: the routine that matters most

Use a soft brush and fluoride toothpaste, angle the bristles towards the gum line and spit, don't rinse, to keep fluoride working.

Sensitivity · 4 min read

Why do my teeth hurt with cold drinks?

Common causes include gum recession, worn enamel and grinding. A check-up helps find the cause; desensitising toothpaste can help some people.

Kids · 5 min read

Your child's first dental visit: a parent's guide

Aim for a visit by their first birthday. Keep it light, avoid words like "hurt", and let them watch you have a check-up first.
